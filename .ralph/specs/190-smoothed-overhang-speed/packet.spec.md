# Packet Contract: 190-smoothed-overhang-speed

## Goal

Replace the whole-entity quantized speed lookup in `modules/core-modules/overhang-classifier-default/src/lib.rs` — today the whole-entity maximum `entity.path.points.iter().filter_map(|p| p.overhang_quartile).max()` **merged with the curl-derived quartile** (`let Some(q) = overhang_q.max(curl_q) else …`, measured in `run_finalization`; the rule being replaced is the **merge**, not the overhang term alone) feeding one `EntityMutation::SetSpeedFactor(overhang_speed(q, config) / base)` — with a per-point port of canonical `ExtrusionQualityEstimator::estimate_extrusion_quality`'s `calculate_speed` lambda: build the six-entry `speed_sections` table from the hardcoded `overhang_overlap_levels` `{90, 75, 50, 25, 13, 0}`, **read each point's `Point3WithWidth.overhang_distance_mm` — the signed, `boundary_offset`-normalised distance stamped by the prepass in packet `193-overhang-distance-prepass-carrier`** — linearly interpolate the speed between bracketing sections, take `min(calculate_speed(curr), calculate_speed(next))` clamped to the role's reference speed, and emit the whole entity as one `EntityMutation::SetPointSpeedFactors` (packet 189's carrier) gated on a new `enable_overhang_speed` config key plus canonical's `!on_first_layer()` condition.

**The distance is read, not computed.** The maintainer ruled **option (C)** on this packet's `[BLOCK]` set: the in-module cross-layer wall-distance scan is dissolved, along with the `+0.5 × outer_wall_line_width` proxy compensation and the `OuterWall`-only-vs-all-roles question that went with it. `ADR-0053` is the decision record. The carrier's signedness and `+ boundary_offset` normalisation are defined **once**, in `.ralph/specs/193-overhang-distance-prepass-carrier/design.md` §Data and Contract Notes §"The signedness contract", and are cited — never restated — here and in packet 191.
