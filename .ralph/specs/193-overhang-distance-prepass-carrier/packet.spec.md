# Packet Contract: 193-overhang-distance-prepass-carrier

## Goal

Give PnP a **continuous, signed, per-point overhang distance** where today it has only a four-bucket quantization: add `overhang_distance_mm: Option<f32>` to `Point3WithWidth` (`crates/slicer-ir/src/slice_ir.rs`) and the matching `overhang-distance-mm: option<f32>` to the `point3-with-width` WIT record (`crates/slicer-schema/wit/deps/types.wit`); carry the previous layer's slice boundary from `annotate_overhangs` (`crates/slicer-core/src/algos/overhang_annotation.rs`) through `SurfaceClassificationIR` and `SliceRegionView` to the two sites that already stamp `overhang_quartile` — `expolygon_to_path3d` (`crates/slicer-core/src/perimeter_utils.rs`, the classic-perimeters path) and the per-vertex band loop in `modules/core-modules/arachne-perimeters/src/lib.rs` (packet 148's AC-5) — and stamp the distance there beside the quartile; then sweep every exhaustive `Point3WithWidth` struct literal in the workspace.

This packet ships the carrier and changes no emitted G-code. It is the prerequisite the maintainer's **option (C)** ruling created for packet 190, and it is where the carrier's **signedness and `+ boundary_offset` normalisation are defined once** for packets 190 and 191 to reference identically.

## Scope Boundaries

Classification stays upstream — that is the half of ADR-0031 option (C) exists to preserve. This packet adds a *second, continuous* signal beside `overhang_quartile`; it does **not** touch `annotate_overhangs`' four concentric quartile bands or `BAND_BOUNDARY_MULTIPLIERS` (`crates/slicer-core/src/algos/overhang_annotation.rs`), does not change any band geometry, and does not read or write any speed. No consumer of `overhang_distance_mm` is written here: `modules/core-modules/overhang-classifier-default/src/lib.rs` is untouched (that is packet 190), and no vertex is inserted, moved or removed (that is packet 191). No config key is added. No `[ir-access]`, `[claims]` or `[stage]` manifest entry changes, so no DAG edge moves.

**What is materially wider than the one-line framing of the queue row, stated rather than discovered mid-implementation.** A continuous distance cannot be recovered from the banded polygons the prepass emits today — that is the same irreversibility argument packet 190's §Problem Statement makes about the quartile. Stamping it therefore requires a *new* prepass→perimeter data path: an additive `SurfaceClassificationIR` field, a `slice-region-view` accessor, and the host marshalling between them. Those are in scope here and are what makes this packet `M`-aggregate rather than `S`. See `design.md` §Code Change Surface, and §Open Questions for the recorded option of splitting this packet in two.
