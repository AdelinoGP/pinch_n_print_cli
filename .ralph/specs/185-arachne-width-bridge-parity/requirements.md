## Problem Statement

Three open deviations sit on the same two functions in `modules/core-modules/arachne-perimeters/src/lib.rs` and are cheapest to close together, because two of them (`arachne_params_from_config`) share an edit site and the third (`build_walls`) shares the same width-domain reasoning.

**D-164 (arachne half).** Canonical registers `outer_wall_line_width` / `inner_wall_line_width` as `coFloatOrPercent` with `ratio_over = "nozzle_diameter"`, `min = 0`, and default `0` — where `0` means *auto-derive from the nozzle*. PnP models both as plain `float` with `min = 0.1`, so neither the auto sentinel nor a percent-of-nozzle profile value is expressible; a profile ported from OrcaSlicer cannot round-trip.

**D-168.** `ArachneParams::default`'s `smallest_line_segment_squared = 0.0025` / `allowed_error_distance_squared = 0.000025` correspond to `0.05 mm` / `0.005 mm`, while canonical `WallToolPathsParams` defaults `wall_maximum_resolution = 0.5 mm` and `wall_maximum_deviation = 0.025 mm`. When the keys are unset, PnP simplifies arachne walls 10×/5× less aggressively than upstream. The deviation row's *stated cause* is separately wrong and must be corrected as part of closing it (see §Cross-packet impact).

**D-163.** Canonical exempts a bridging overhang perimeter from the spacing→width back-conversion at emission, substituting the bridge flow wholesale. PnP's `build_walls` converts uniformly, so a bridge vertex carries a beading-derived width where canonical carries the bridge thread's diameter.

This is one coherent slice: all three are arachne flow/width parity, all three land in two functions plus one manifest, and all three are gated by the same `ArachneParams::default()`-driven test population. No packet is reopened or superseded.
