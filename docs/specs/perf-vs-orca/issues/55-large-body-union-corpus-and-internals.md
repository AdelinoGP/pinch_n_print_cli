## Answer — measured resolution, 2026-10-07

**The native sweep contains concrete unchanged-edge projection recomputation.
Recommend scoping a scanbeam-local `TopX` cache, not removing required unions.**
Measured attribution, exact-corpus coverage, pinned dependency provenance and
limits live in [Large body-union findings](../evidence/t55-body-union/FINDINGS.md).
The usage-limit interruption did not require repeating completed slices;
continuation inspected saved progress and completed the outstanding diagnosis
and validation.

- All eight selected full-original-argument `build_roles` cases replay exactly,
  including the largest requests, five hole-bearing union outputs and four
  body-changing interface cuts. Both modes' exact captures agree. Vertex,
  hole and role negative controls reject altered expectations. This closes the
  previously identified selected-case coverage gaps, not all-layer replay.
- Eight fresh baseline/probe slices are clean and nonvacuous, with actual
  streamed byte equality in all four cells. Existing role separation, integer
  ring order, carve repair and downstream body subtraction are preserved.
- Native internal work is the sweep, not flattening or output reconstruction.
  In the timer-only selected base layer 9 request, execute is 417,204,300 ns
  inside the 420,270,500 ns wrapper; intersection-list build and scanbeam-top
  processing are nested children, not additional cost. These are instrumented
  single-request observations, not production latency or savings estimates.
- Separate unchanged-edge counters find 14,142,214 repeated `TopX` projections
  on base layer 9 and 14,647,003 on layer 21, with zero projection-value
  mismatches in all selected cases. The diagnostic snapshots never replace
  the existing calculations. Exact polygon deduplication is falsified as a
  useful lead for this corpus: selected carved inputs contain no exact duplicates.

The concrete next proposal is a separate scanbeam-local saved projection for
unchanged edges, **not** reuse of mutable intersection `curr_x`. It needs edge/y
identity and invalidation on geometry, slope, vertex replacement and recycling,
with unchanged-calculation fallback. Added state and access may erase the gain;
impact is unmeasured. Dependency integration and the exactness/speed acceptance
