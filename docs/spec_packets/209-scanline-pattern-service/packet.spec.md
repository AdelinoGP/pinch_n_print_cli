# Packet Contract: 209-scanline-pattern-service

> **Slug is historical.** The directory name dates from a superseded approach (a shared
> `slicer_core::scanline_fill` kernel / pattern service). That approach is **forbidden** by
> `docs/adr/0026-infill-linking-algorithms-in-linker-module.md` and has been removed from this
> packet in full — see `requirements.md` §"Re-scope 2026-08-07". The packet now **reconciles the
> three copies in place**. The directory is not renamed because renaming a packet mid-queue breaks
> `docs/specs/deviation-remediation-206-212-plan.md`'s row reference.

## Goal

Make the three independent scan-line fill copies — `scan_expolygon`
(`modules/core-modules/rectilinear-infill/src/lib.rs`), `TraditionalSupport::fill_expolygon`
(`modules/core-modules/traditional-support/src/lib.rs`) and `SupportSurfaceIroning::fill_expolygon`
(`modules/core-modules/support-surface-ironing/src/lib.rs`) — **agree on one canonical scan-line
behaviour**, each keeping its own implementation, so the correctness divergence between them is
removed while the duplication itself is left to a future WIT pattern-services packet.

## Scope Boundaries

In: reconciling four behaviour axes **in place** across the three copies — the vertex/edge inclusion
test, the rotation reference point, the scan start, and the scan grid extent (including the
sub-spacing case) — with each axis decided against canonical `fill_surface_by_lines` /
`slice_region_by_vertical_lines` (`FillRectilinear.cpp`); deleting `traditional-support`'s centroid
fallback and `rectilinear-infill`'s top-boundary post-pass and sub-spacing bail as consequences of
those decisions; re-baselining the self-captured fixtures the canonical corrections move; correcting
`adjust_solid_spacing`'s false port attribution **in place** in `rectilinear-infill`; adding
geometric-invariant test coverage to `traditional-support` and `support-surface-ironing`, neither of
which has any today; amending DEV-127 (it stays **Open**) and filing five `D-209-*` rows; and
correcting ADR-0009's three rotted pointers **without touching any normative ADR content**.

Out — and these are hard prohibitions, not preferences:

- **Any extraction of scan-line or fill-pattern logic into `slicer-core`.** No
  `crates/slicer-core/src/scanline_fill.rs`, no `scanline_fill`, no `ScanlineFillParams`, no
  `FillSegment`, no `slicer_core::patterns`, no `slicer_core::infill_ops`, and no promotion of
  `adjust_solid_spacing` out of `rectilinear-infill`. Forbidden by ADR-0026 (§Decision, §Consequences,
  §Future-Reviewer Notes) and by ADR-0009's Future-Reviewer Note. See `design.md` §"ADR conformance
  check". AC-2's guard clause enforces this mechanically.
- **Any amendment to ADR-0009 or ADR-0026.** The user-approved 2026-08-07 override of ADR-0009's
  Future-Reviewer Note **lapses unused** — with no extraction there is nothing to override. Only the
  three factual pointers in ADR-0009 are corrected (AC-15), and AC-15 asserts no `## Amendment`
  section appears.
- **De-duplication.** Three copies remain after this packet. Nothing in any artifact may claim
  otherwise. DEV-127 stays Open (AC-13).
- Any WIT, IR, `ResolvedConfig`, manifest or schema-version change; any new host service.
- Canonical's `align_to_grid` / `_infill_direction` anchor model and its `full_infill()`
  `(line_spacing + SCALED_EPSILON) / 2` half-spacing inset. Both are real, separable parity gaps;
  both are recorded in `D-209-HALF-OPEN-SCAN-GRID-ADOPTED` rather than ported.
- Canonical's neighbour-product tangency filter as literal code. Its *crossing-count* effect is
  ported (AC-3); its *tangential-drop* effect is approximated by the half-open predicate plus the
  zero-length-span rule, which is coverage-equivalent but splits one span into two abutting spans at
  an interior tangential touch. Recorded as `D-209-TANGENTIAL-TOUCH-SPAN-SPLIT`.
- Density-unit reconciliation (infill 0–1 ratio, support 0–100 percent), role/width/output-sink
  selection, `pattern_shift` (infill-only), and non-rectilinear patterns.

only; never by line number.

## Shipped infill geometry moves — this is deliberate and owned

Adopting canonical's half-open grid **changes `rectilinear-infill` output**. This is chosen over
deferral because `CLAUDE.md` §Test Discipline puts canonical parity above a green self-captured
fixture, because the packet cannot reconcile three copies onto a grid that none of them shares with
canonical, and because the prior draft's excuses for deferring do not survive measurement: the
half-open **extent** is separable from the `full_infill()` inset (`make_fill_lines` runs the same
`ceil(w/s)` bound with no inset — the inset is applied only by `fill_surface_by_lines` under
`params.full_infill()`), and "re-baselining would weaken an AC" inverts Test Discipline. Note the
limit of the first point: it does **not** show the bound is separable from `align_to_grid`.
`make_fill_lines` merges `align_to_grid(bounding_box.min, Point(line_spacing, line_spacing), refpt)`
into the bbox *before* reading `min.x()`, so canonical's grid **origin** is grid-anchored on that
path. PnP adopts the extent and anchors the origin at the surface bbox min — canonical's behaviour in
the one branch that skips `align_to_grid` (`full_infill() && !dont_adjust`) — and `align_to_grid`
stays an Open, unported gap. See `design.md` §"Scan grid extent" for the exact wording that must be
carried into `D-209-HALF-OPEN-SCAN-GRID-ADOPTED`.

Two self-captured fixtures are re-baselined to canonical-correct output. **Both re-baselines are
count changes justified by canonical; no assertion may be weakened, narrowed or deleted.**

- `square_10mm_density_20_emits_n_raw_segments`
  (`modules/core-modules/rectilinear-infill/tests/rectilinear_raw_emit_tdd.rs`): 10 mm square,
  2 mm spacing. Today `floor(10/2) + 1 = 6` (five main-loop rows plus the post-pass top edge).
  Canonical `ceil(10/2) = 5`. The expression changes from `floor() as usize + 1` to `ceil() as usize`;
  every other assertion in the test (2 points per path, points on the square boundary, no shared
  endpoints) stays byte-identical.
- `very_small_polygon_emits_no_paths_without_panic`
  (`modules/core-modules/rectilinear-infill/tests/rectilinear_infill_edge_cases_tdd.rs`): a 0.1 mm
  square at 0.8 mm spacing asserts **zero** paths, which is the sub-spacing bail. Canonical emits
  `ceil(0.1/0.8) = 1` line. Renamed to `very_small_polygon_emits_one_scan_row_without_panic` and
  re-baselined to exactly one path, keeping the no-panic assertion.

Recorded as `D-209-HALF-OPEN-SCAN-GRID-ADOPTED`.
